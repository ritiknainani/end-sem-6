## 3. High-Priority Preparation Tiers (Exam Survival Strategy)

To score 70+ out of 80 marks in MU Semester 7 MIS, students must master:

### Tier 1: Non-Negotiable Core (Guarantees 55-60 Marks)
1. **Information Security, CIA Triad & Controls (10-15 Marks):**
   - Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability principles.
   - Comprehensive 3-tier security controls: Physical (biometrics, locks), Access (authentication, authorization, ACLs), Communication/Network (firewalls, encryption, VPN, IDS/IPS).
   - Analysis of Computer Crimes (financial gain, insider threats, hacktivism).
2. **Cloud Computing Service & Deployment Models (10 Marks):**
   - Service models: IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, CaaS (Definitions, responsibilities, enterprise examples).
   - Deployment models: Public, Private, Hybrid, Community Cloud.
3. **Data Warehousing, Data Marts & Big Data (10 Marks):**
   - Centralized EDW vs Decentralized Data Marts (Subject-oriented, Integrated, Time-variant, Non-volatile).
   - Star Schema vs Snowflake Schema.
   - Big Data 5 V's (Volume, Velocity, Variety, Veracity, Value) and business monetization.
4. **Knowledge Management & Nonaka's SECI Model (10 Marks):**
   - Tacit Knowledge (Intuition, experience) vs Explicit Knowledge (Documents, code).
   - SECI Spiral: Socialization (Tacit $\to$ Tacit), Externalization (Tacit $\to$ Explicit), Combination (Explicit $\to$ Explicit), Internalization (Explicit $\to$ Tacit).
   - KM Lifecycle: Create $\to$ Capture $\to$ Refine $\to$ Store $\to$ Manage $\to$ Disseminate.
5. **Business Intelligence & Decision Support Systems (DSS / ESS) (10 Marks):**
   - Decision-Making Hierarchy: Strategic (Executive), Tactical (Middle Management), Operational (Frontline).
   - DSS Components: Data Management, Model Management, User Interface, Knowledge Base.
   - ESS capabilities: Drill-down, trend analysis, KPI dashboards.
6. **Functional Area IS (FAIS) & Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) (10 Marks):**
   - Core functional systems: Accounting IS, Finance IS, POM IS, Marketing IS, HR IS.
   - ERP architecture: Central database eliminating corporate data silos; ERP implementation challenges (cost, culture change).
   - Reporting taxonomy: Scheduled, Exception, Drill-down, Key-Indicator reports.

### Tier 2: High-Yield Theory & Case Studies (20-25 Marks)
1. **E-Commerce & Mobile Commerce (10 Marks):**
   - B2B, B2C, C2C business models; Electronic payment systems.
   - M-Commerce drivers and constraints (Form factor, security, cellular bandwidth).
   - Architecture of a social-computing delivery application (e.g., food/grocery logistics).
2. **Social Computing in Business & Web 1.0 vs 2.0 vs 3.0 (10 Marks):**
   - Web 1.0 (Static, Read-Only) $\to$ Web 2.0 (Dynamic, Read-Write, Social) $\to$ Web 3.0 (Decentralized, Semantic, Read-Write-Own).
   - Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Operational vs Analytical vs Collaborative CRM.
   - Risks of social computing (Brand reputation damage, data leaks, employee time loss).
3. **System Development & Tools Augmenting Traditional SDLC (10 Marks):**
   - SDLC phases: Feasibility $\to$ Analysis $\to$ Design $\to$ Implementation $\to$ Testing $\to$ Maintenance.
   - Modern augmentation tools: Agile Scrum, Computer-Aided Software Engineering (CASE) tools, Prototyping, DevOps CI/CD pipelines, Low-Code/No-Code platforms.
4. **Hospital Management Information System (HMIS) Design Case Study (10 Marks):**
   - Complete system blueprint: Patient Registration, EHR, Pharmacy Inventory, Billing, Diagnostic Labs, Bed Allocation, Executive KPI Dashboard.
5. **Messaging App Comparison: WhatsApp vs Telegram (5 Marks):**
   - 6-parameter matrix: Multimedia sharing limits, Security (E2EE by default vs opt-in secret chats), Privacy, Cloud storage vs local backups, Channels/Bots, Cross-platform notifications.
