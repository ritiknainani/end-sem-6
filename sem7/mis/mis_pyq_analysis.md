## 1. Executive Summary & Syllabus Module Architecture

Management Information Systems (MIS) in Mumbai University Semester 7 follows a structured 6-module curriculum. Analysis of all seven consecutive examination cycles confirms extreme consistency across core areas: Information Security controls and authentication, Cloud Computing architectures, Knowledge Management lifecycles (LMS/SECI), Functional Area Information Systems (FAIS/ERP), and SDLC paradigms.

## 3. High-Priority Preparation Tiers (7-Cycle Calibrated)

### Tier 1: Non-Negotiable Core (Guarantees 65+ Marks)
1. **Information Security, Controls & User Authentication (20-30 Marks):**
   - The CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability).
   - Security Controls: General vs Application Controls (Physical, Access, Cryptographic, Administrative).
   - User Authentication: Passwords, Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA/2FA), Biometrics, Single Sign-On (SSO), and Usability vs Productivity trade-off.
   - Privacy Invasions: Cookies, Web Bugs (Beacons), Spyware, Adware.
   - Analysis of Computer Crimes: Financial gain, corporate espionage, hacktivism, insider threats.
2. **Functional Area Information Systems (FAIS) & ERP (15-20 Marks):**
   - Detailed support provided by Accounting IS, Finance IS, POM IS, Marketing IS, and Human Resources IS.
   - Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP): Centralized database eliminating data silos; cross-functional business process orchestration.
3. **Data Management, Warehouses, Marts & Big Data Governance (10-15 Marks):**
   - Data Warehouse vs Data Mart: Architecture, Star vs Snowflake schema, ETL pipeline.
   - Big Data Paradox: Why storage volume is not the real issue; data governance, ethical use, access control, and security are the true operational bottlenecks.
4. **Knowledge Management & LMS Framework (10 Marks):**
   - Tacit Knowledge vs Explicit Knowledge; Nonaka's SECI spiral model.
   - Knowledge Management Lifecycle (Create $\to$ Capture $\to$ Refine $\to$ Store $\to$ Manage $\to$ Disseminate) applied directly to construct a Learning Management System (LMS) framework.
5. **Cloud Computing & Web Evolution (10-15 Marks):**
   - Service models: IaaS, PaaS, SaaS, CaaS (Definitions, user control boundaries, real-world examples).
   - Deployment models: Public, Private, Hybrid, Community Cloud.
   - Web Evolution: Web 1.0 (Static Read-Only) vs Web 2.0 (Social Read-Write) vs Web 3.0 (Semantic Read-Write-Own).
6. **Business Strategy & SDLC Paradigms (10-15 Marks):**
   - Concept of the Informed User and its competitive value.
   - Business Processes in MIS: Definition, integration, and cross-functional workflow.
   - Porter's 5 Competitive Forces & Generic Strategies for Competitive Advantage.
   - SDLC: Planning, System Analysis, System Design, Implementation, Testing, and Maintenance.
